# Cerlock brand notes

## The name

Cerlock is Sherlock with the "Sh" swapped for the C in CS. A demo is a case: every round leaves evidence, and Cerlock finds it and shows you where to look. The detective idea lives in the logo and in a few names (Evidence, Ballistics, Briefing). The look itself is modern and sharp, closer to the game than to an old book.

## What it should feel like

Like a tool a pro team would actually keep open next to CS2. Clean, dark, fast and precise. It should feel related to the game's own HUD (same icons, similar type) without copying it, and it should not look like a generic dashboard template or a neon esports overlay.

## Principles

1. **The game is the hero.** The map, the players and the utility get the contrast and the colour. The interface around them stays calm and dark.
2. **Real game language.** Weapons, grenades, armor, the bomb and killfeed modifiers use the real CS2 icons, never emoji or made up symbols. Names and numbers are short, like in game.
3. **Conclusions first, numbers second.** Say "deaths are not getting traded", then show the 13%.
4. **Every claim has evidence.** Anything Cerlock says links to the moment in the demo it came from.

## Voice

Short sentences and plain words. Dry, never jokey. It should read like notes from a coach who watched the demo twice.

- Good: "Flashed a teammate, who died 0.8s later."
- Avoid: "Oops! Looks like someone forgot to communicate!"

## Logo

The mark is the C of Cerlock drawn as the lens of a magnifying glass, with the handle coming out of the opening of the C and a red dot where a crosshair would be. The wordmark is "cerlock" in Barlow Semi Condensed Bold, lowercase.

## Type

- **Barlow** for all running text and tables.
- **Barlow Semi Condensed** (600 to 700) for player and team names, scores, the clock, headings and small uppercase labels.
- Numbers use tabular figures so columns line up. No monospace fonts, no serifs.

## Icons

Game icons come from the CS2 files (weapons, grenades, armor, kit, bomb, bombsite letters, killfeed modifiers like headshot, wallbang, no-scope, through smoke and blind). They are drawn as single colour silhouettes, white or tinted with the team colour, at sizes that match the text next to them. Interface icons (play, pause, layers) are simple 2px line icons.

## The map

The radar image is shown crisp and cool, not tinted warm. Heights stay readable through light and dark. The playable area gets a thin bright outline and a soft shadow so it lifts off the background, and a faint grid sits behind it. Callouts are small uppercase labels with a dark halo. Bombsites are marked with the game's A and B badges.

Players are round tokens in their team colour with a small pointer for where they look, a white number for their slot, and a thin health ring when they are hurt. The followed player gets a red ring. Utility uses the real grenade icons and clear shapes: soft grey smoke clouds with a countdown ring, orange fire, a white pop for flashes.

## Shapes

- 6px corners on panels and cards, 4px on buttons and chips.
- 1px borders instead of heavy shadows, one soft shadow for floating things (tooltips, popovers).
- Dense but not cramped: 8px spacing grid.
